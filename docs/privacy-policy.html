Keyscape - Privacy Policy

Effective date: 9 October 2026 · App: Keyscape (package com.keyscape.app)

Keyscape does not collect, store on any server, share or sell any personal or sensitive user data.

What the app stores, and where

  • Your optional display name, lesson progress, scores, streaks, achievements, favourite songs and settings are saved only on your device, in the app's private storage.
  • MIDI files you choose to save under "My songs" are copied into the app's private storage on your device so you can play them again; files you play without saving are kept only until the app closes. They are never uploaded.
  • Android may include this local data in your personal device backup (Google account backup) if you have enabled backups. That backup is controlled by you and Google; Keyscape cannot read it.

What the app does not do

  • No internet access: the app does not request the INTERNET permission, so it cannot send data anywhere.
  • No accounts, no sign-in, no advertising, no analytics, no crash reporting SDKs and no tracking of any kind.
  • No access to your contacts, location, camera, microphone, photos or files other than a MIDI file you explicitly pick.

MIDI keyboards

If you connect a MIDI keyboard, the notes you play are processed in real time on your device to produce sound and check your playing. They are not stored, except if you press Record; recordings are kept in memory only and discarded when you close the app.

Children

Keyscape is suitable for all ages and collects no data from anyone, including children.

Deleting your data

Use Settings → Reset all progress in the app, or uninstall the app. Either removes all data the app has stored.
