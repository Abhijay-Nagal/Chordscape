## Full description (max 4000 characters)
Keyscape is a complete piano school in your pocket - from your very first note to Chopin, jazz and beyond.

STRUCTURED COURSES
• 14 courses and 172 interactive lessons: Piano Foundations, Music Theory A-Z, Classical, Romantic, Jazz, Pop & Modern, Blues & Boogie, Gospel, Latin, Film & Neo-classical, Improvisation, Ear Training, Sight-Reading and Technique.
• Learn octaves, reading music, rhythm, scales, chords, chord progressions, cadences, modes, harmony and form - every idea shown on the keyboard and the staff, and every sound playable.
• Every unit ends with a test. All lessons are open from day one, so you can skip what you already know.
• Portraits of the great composers and historical instruments bring music history to life.

COMPLETE PIECES, NOT SIMPLIFIED SNIPPETS
• 222 pieces, including 184 complete scores from Bach, Handel, Scarlatti, Haydn, Mozart, Beethoven, Schubert, Chopin, Schumann, Liszt, Brahms, Grieg, Tchaikovsky, Debussy, Satie, Rachmaninoff, Joplin and more - Für Elise, the Moonlight Sonata, Clair de Lune, the Fantaisie-Impromptu, Schubert's Impromptus, the Maple Leaf Rag and many more. Every song is complete - no snippets.
• Composer pages with illustrated storybooks about their lives, timelines and quizzes.
• Choose Easy, Medium or Hard for every piece.
• Three ways to see the notes: Waterfall, Fallen Note (falling balls) or scrolling Sheet Music.
• Note Wait (the music waits for you), Listen and Practice (scored) modes; right hand, left hand or both; 10%-200% speed; A-B loop with the looped section highlighted; metronome; a sustain pedal that follows the score.
• Mark favourites, and upload your own MIDI files to "My songs".

A REAL PIANO FEEL
• Recorded concert grand piano plus 29 more sounds - electric pianos, harpsichord, harp, guitars, celesta, vibraphone, marimba, bells, organs, strings, choir, flute and synths.
• Sound engineering: reverb and room size, pedal sustain, touch sensitivity, EQ, chorus, drive, tremolo, transpose, tuning and pitch bend.
• Play on screen with up to 88 keys, or connect a digital piano or MIDI keyboard via USB.
• Record and replay your playing.

PRACTICE TOOLS
Chord Explorer, Scale & Mode Explorer, interactive Circle of Fifths, Progression Player, Metronome, Ear Trainer, Note Reading Trainer, Chord Finder and a Theory Encyclopedia of 200+ terms.

PRIVATE BY DESIGN
No account, no ads, no tracking - the app has no internet access at all. Your progress stays on your device.

Music credits: complete scores from the Mutopia Project (public domain / Creative Commons); grand piano samples: Salamander Grand Piano by Alexander Holm (CC BY 3.0). Full credits are in the app under More → About.

# Data safety form answers
1. **Does your app collect or share any of the required user data types?** → **No**
2. Is all of the user data collected by your app encrypted in transit? → (not shown when you answer No)
3. Do you provide a way for users to request that their data is deleted? → Not applicable (nothing collected); you may mention "Settings → Reset all progress".

Why "No" is correct: the app has no internet permission and no SDKs that send data. Data kept only on the device (progress, imported MIDI files) is not "collected" in Google's definition.
